# CS 330 Portfolio Reflection

## How do I approach designing software?

When I begin designing software, I like to first understand the overall goal of the project before then focusing on individual components. Breaking a larger project into smaller tasks makes the work more manageable and helps me stay organized. For my 3D scene, I started by selecting a reference image and identifying the objects that would need to be created. From there, I planned how each object could be built using basic 3D shapes before combining everything into a complete scene.

## What new design skills has your work on the project helped you to craft?

This project helped me develop stronger spatial reasoning and visualization skills. I learned how to recreate real-world objects using primitive shapes while paying attention to proportions, placement, textures, lighting, and camera positioning. I also gained experience thinking about how individual design choices contribute to the overall appearance of a scene.

## What design process did you follow for your project work?

My design process consisted of planning the scene, creating the basic geometry, adding textures, implementing lighting, and then refining each component through testing. Each milestone allowed me to improve the previous version until the final scene closely matched my original reference image.

## How could tactics from your design approach be applied in future work?

Breaking projects into smaller steps, planning before coding, and refining features through multiple revisions are strategies that can be applied to many software development projects. These techniques help reduce errors and make larger projects easier to manage.

## How do I approach developing programs?

When developing programs, I focus on implementing one feature at a time and testing frequently to ensure everything works before moving on. I find that solving smaller problems individually makes debugging much easier than trying to complete everything at once.

## What new development strategies did you use while working on your 3D scene?

Throughout this project, I became more comfortable working with transformations, textures, lighting, and reusable functions to organize my code. I also learned the importance of making small adjustments and testing them immediately rather than changing many things at once.

## How did iteration factor into your development?

Iteration was one of the most important parts of this project. Every milestone involved revisiting previous work, making improvements, and correcting issues with object placement, scaling, textures, and lighting. Repeated testing allowed me to gradually improve both the visual quality and organization of my scene.

## How has your approach to developing code evolved throughout the milestones, which led you to the project’s completion?

As I progressed through the milestones, I became much more confident working with OpenGL and understanding how different parts of the graphics pipeline work together. Early in the course, I relied heavily on trial and error, but by the final project I was able to make purposeful adjustments to transformations, materials, textures, and lighting while keeping my code organized and easier to maintain.

## How can computer science help me in reaching my goals?

Computer science provides the technical foundation needed to pursue a career in software development. The problem-solving, programming, and analytical skills developed through this degree prepare me to build reliable software and continue learning new technologies throughout my career.

## How do computational graphics and visualizations give you new knowledge and skills that can be applied in your future educational pathway?

Computational graphics introduced me to concepts such as coordinate systems, transformations, texture mapping, lighting, and rendering. These topics strengthen my understanding of mathematics and programming while providing experience that can be applied to future computer science courses involving graphics, simulations, and visualization.

## How do computational graphics and visualizations give you new knowledge and skills that can be applied in your future professional pathway?

This course provided practical experience using C++, OpenGL, and graphics programming concepts that are used in game development, simulation software, engineering visualization, and interactive applications. Beyond graphics itself, the project reinforced valuable professional skills such as planning, debugging, iterative development, and attention to detail, all of which are important in software engineering careers.
